Rope Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a rope cutting job with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the pieces a spool cuts, the spools the job takes, the cost a piece, and the seconds the cuts take. It uses no named constants. The meters, the piece length, the spool yield, the spool price and the cutting speed are yours to decide and read off your own spools, bench and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your rope, your knife or your knots and does not know whether the rope will really measure out, the ends will fray or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a spool is worth its price or a rope is right for a load. Every number here is your own entry.

The pieces truth

Pieces: the meters divided by the meters a piece, rounded down, with the meters left over.

The spools truth

Spools: the pieces divided by the pieces a spool, rounded up, with what the last spool gives.

The cost truth

Cost: the spools times the spool price, to the cent, and spread over the pieces.

The time truth

Time: the cuts times the seconds a cut, in seconds and in minutes and seconds.

Rope Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
